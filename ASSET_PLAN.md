# ASSET PLAN
## Otter characters
8x otter spritesheets (idle/swim/throw/hit/celebrate), 256x256 frame area, 6-10 frames per anim.
## Enemies
Fish, eel, crab, turtle, gull, catfish boss. 192x192 recommended.
## Collectibles
fish, crayfish, mussel, clam, snail, frog, shell, pebble, bubble, heart leaf, golden fish, pearl, shield, magnet, star. 128x128.
## Backgrounds
Sky, riverbank, 5 water layers, light rays, riverbed props; parallax tiles 1024x512.
## UI
Buttons, cards, HUD icons (heart/oxygen/score/pebble). 64/128 px variants.
## VFX
Splash, sparkle, hit puff, combo pop, checkpoint burst.
## Tiles/decorations
Stones, reeds, logs, flowers, shells, sediment.
## Animations
Swim loop, throw, damage bounce, collect pulse, enemy retreat spin.
